# Planning

Planning is a local daily planner for personal tasks. Enable it in Settings → Planning, then arrange it through Overview. Standard and Focus layouts can show it independently. Classic and Glass surfaces are available.

## Make a daily plan

Add a task directly in the widget and press Enter. Today, Tomorrow and Later choose where a task goes when no date is typed. You can also choose a date to plan ahead or look back at completed tasks.

The capture field understands the same English calendar-date grammar as Google Tasks. “Call Mum tomorrow” becomes a task called “Call Mum” planned for tomorrow. “Book tickets in 3 days” uses three calendar days from today, even when you are viewing a different day. The interpreted title and date appear before you add it. Weekdays and explicit dates also work. Put the whole title in double quotes to keep date words as literal text. Competing or invalid dates are not submitted.

Click a task's text to change its title, notes, day or estimate. The Date or phrase field accepts “today”, “tomorrow”, “in 3 days” or an explicit date; leave it blank to keep the task in Later. Changes are saved only when you choose Save task or use a reorder button. Cancel or Escape discards the edit.

Star up to three tasks for a day's priorities. They appear above the rest of that day's plans. Check a task to mark it done; completed tasks remain available in the Done section and can be reopened. Reorder controls move tasks within their day and priority/completion group.

## Keep the plan realistic

Estimated minutes are optional. Set Daily planning time in Settings to compare those estimates with a daily target. A target of 0 disables the comparison. Tasks without estimates do not contribute to planned minutes. Exceeding the target is informational and does not block tasks.

Unfinished tasks from earlier dates appear under Unfinished from earlier when viewing Today. Choose the arrow to bring one into today, or edit it to choose another day or Later. Nothing is silently rolled forward. Moving a task to another day clears its old priority, so today's priorities are a fresh choice.

## Storage and recovery

Plans work offline and are stored on this browser profile. They are not synchronized with Google Tasks or another device. Moo Backups include task records, estimates, styling and the daily time target. A full profile reset restores empty Planning defaults.

Planning retains up to 500 tasks, including completed tasks. There is no automatic deletion of history. Settings → Planning offers a confirmed deletion of completed tasks across all days; unfinished tasks are kept. This history cleanup cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you want to retain those records. Delete tasks you no longer need; deletion asks for confirmation and offers Undo until another task is deleted or the widget is closed. Other tabs receive saved changes. A stale edit is rejected rather than overwriting newer work. Unreadable saved data is preserved and blocks editing until a valid backup is restored.

The new Planning interface is translated into English and Bulgarian. Other selectable interface languages currently use English for these new strings. Natural date input is English regardless of the selected interface language.
